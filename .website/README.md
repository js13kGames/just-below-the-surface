As the intrepid archaeologist witch Dot Vine, you have to scour the tropics to find the 13 unicorn bones!

Controls, any of:
- Gamepad: Dpad, South, West
- Arrows, Z, X
- WASD, Space, Left Shift

Cast the Spell of Revealing with your wand to summon a rainbow that circles the nearest bone. Then use your shovel to dig it up.

I've also made a Deluxe CD-ROM Edition -- so big it doesn't even fit on a floppy disk! That's linked here as the Director's Cut.
